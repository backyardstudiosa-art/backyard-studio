# Backyard Studio Content Realignment Report

## Removed
- Interior styling and space-reset positioning from the public entry page and `/studio/` homepage.
- Keepsake, reflection-tool, personal-renewal, and healing-led service positioning from About, Services, Contact, and Journal pages.
- Collection/resource navigation labels that implied the previous creative-products model.
- Journal category and hero language that framed the studio around healing, devotional support, or life-transition guidance.

## Rewritten
- `/studio/` now introduces Backyard Studio as a Multimedia Editorial Studio with the four pillars: Strategy, Storytelling, Editorial Design, and Creative Direction.
- About is now an Our Story page centred on evolution, clarification, and refinement: “The heart hasn’t changed. The focus has.”
- Services now presents only the four approved service pillars and the calm client experience.
- Contact is now framed around beginning a thoughtful creative conversation.
- Journal is now positioned as Studio Journal notes on clarity, story, design, and thoughtful creative practice.

## Retained
- Existing static-site structure and navigation functionality.
- Existing contact form foundation.
- Existing genuine visual archive assets for selected work previews.
- Existing typography foundation using an elegant serif and clean sans-serif pairing.

## Extracted from the company-profile direction supplied in the brief
- “Multimedia Editorial Studio.”
- “Thoughtful creativity. Beautifully communicated.”
- Values: Respect, Clarity, Care, Integrity, Longevity.
- Service pillars: Strategy, Storytelling, Editorial Design, Creative Direction.
- Process: Discover → Shape → Design → Deliver.
- Promise language around honest communication, careful listening, thoughtful creation, respect for every story, quality over speed, and trusted creative partnerships.
- Final positioning around communicating meaningful ideas with clarity, confidence, and purpose.
