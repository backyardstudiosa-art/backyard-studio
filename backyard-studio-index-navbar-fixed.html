Backyard Studio partners with founders, brands, creators, and organisations to shape meaningful ideas through strategy, storytelling, design, and creative direction.

From personalised storybooks and keepsakes to brand storytelling and visual design, we bring thoughtful ideas to life with care, clarity, and respect for every story entrusted to us.

We believe every story deserves to be met with respect, shaped with care, and shared with clarity.

What we do

Thoughtful creative partnership

We help you work out what you want to say, shape the story, create the visuals, and bring the pieces together into a thoughtful finished project.

This can mean a family storybook, clearer business messaging, promotional materials, or a creative project shaped around your idea.

How we work

With respect, clarity & care

What we do

Four disciplines. One thoughtful practice.

Our work brings together the thinking, words, visuals, and direction needed to turn meaningful ideas into coherent creative experiences.

01

Strategy

Clarifying positioning, messaging, priorities, and creative direction before design begins.

We help you work out what you want to say, what makes your idea distinctive, and what to do next.

Examples: business messaging, project planning, and creative concepts.

02

Storytelling

Shaping brand stories, editorial writing, and meaningful communication people can connect with.

We turn memories, experiences, and ideas into stories and words people can connect with.

Examples: personalised storybooks, family histories, brand stories, and website writing.

03

Design

Creating thoughtful visual and editorial experiences that communicate with clarity and distinction.

We shape the look and layout of your message so it is clear, engaging, and ready to share.

Examples: illustrated books, promotional graphics, brochures, and digital publications.

04

Creative Direction

Bringing strategy, story, and design together into one cohesive creative outcome.

We bring the planning, words, and visuals together so every part feels connected.

Examples: a complete storybook, a coordinated campaign, or a brand launch.

Our philosophy

Thoughtful before hurried. Meaningful before loud.

In a world filled with constant noise and fast content, Backyard Studio chooses a more intentional approach. Every creative decision should have purpose, every story should be treated with respect, and every piece of work should have reason to exist.

Strategy before design Story before promotion Quality before quantity Relationships before transactions Timeless before trendy
