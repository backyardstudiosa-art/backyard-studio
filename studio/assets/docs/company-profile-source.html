About Backyard Studio

Calm, cohesive homes without a full renovation.

Backyard Studio is a boutique interior styling studio for homeowners who want their rooms to feel calmer, more elegant and more intentional. We focus on atmosphere: the visual balance, comfort and considered details that help a home feel beautifully lived in.

Our work supports transformation without full renovation. Through visual editing, furniture arrangement, lighting, textiles, layout and styling, we help disconnected rooms feel cohesive and restorative.

The process is warm and personal, shaped around how you live and what you already own.
