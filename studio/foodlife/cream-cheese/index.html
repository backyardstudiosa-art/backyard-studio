Karé's kitchen note
Fresh lemon juice is a good place to start. If the milk does not separate clearly, a little additional lemon juice may help. Add it gradually.

Equipment

  • Saucepan
  • Sieve and a bowl
  • Clean cheesecloth or muslin
  • Blender or food processor
  • Clean container with a lid
Optional flavour ideas
Try a pinch of garlic powder, dried herbs or chilli. Blend these in with the salt to suit your taste.

Storage and food safety
Keep the cream cheese refrigerated at 4°C or colder in a clean, airtight container. Use within 1 week as a conservative guideline, and discard it sooner if you notice mould, an unusual smell or other signs of spoilage. Do not leave it sitting at room temperature for extended periods.

Allow extra time for draining and chilling. The 10-minute preparation and cooking estimate does not include these steps.
