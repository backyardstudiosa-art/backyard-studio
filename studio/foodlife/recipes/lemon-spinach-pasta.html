Let's make it

Four easy steps · about 20 minutes

01

Cook the pasta

Bring a large pot of salted water to the boil. Cook the pasta according to the packet instructions until al dente. Before draining, save the pasta water.

02

Wake up the flavour

Warm the olive oil in a large pan over medium-low heat. Add finely chopped garlic and a little lemon zest. Cook for 30–60 seconds, without browning the garlic.

03

Add the greens

Stir in the spinach until wilted. Add the drained pasta, the juice of half the lemon and a splash of pasta water. Toss to coat.

04

Make it silky & serve

Turn the heat low or off. Toss through the parmesan, adding more pasta water a little at a time until glossy. Taste, add more lemon if you like, and season with black pepper and salt.

And that's dinner.

Serve straight away with extra parmesan, black pepper and a little lemon zest.
