The idea

Some of the most meaningful stories begin with ordinary things.

A familiar house. A favourite mug. Someone singing in the kitchen. A notebook left open on the table. Sunday afternoon visitors arriving through the gate.

These moments rarely announce themselves as important. Yet years later, they can be the very things we remember.

Backyard Stories turns those memories into something beautifully written and illustrated.

A Backyard Stories example

The House That Remembered

What happens when an ordinary family home becomes the keeper of a family's memories?

The House That Remembered follows a modern South African family through the everyday rituals that make a house feel like home.

Sunday baking. Video nights. Christmas traditions. Family gatherings. Holidays at the coast. Growing up beneath an old tree.

And eventually, the quiet realisation that even when a family leaves a house, they don't necessarily leave the memories behind.

Ordinary family life can become extraordinary when it is remembered through storytelling.
The house

A lived-in home, an established garden, a familiar gate and a tree that has seen it all.

The rituals

Coffee, baking, notebooks, music, video nights and Sunday gatherings.

The traditions

Christmas, the braai, family recipes, holidays and the people who always came back.

The memories

The things that remain long after the moment itself has passed.

This is an example of what a Backyard Story might become.
